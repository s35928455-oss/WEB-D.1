Mirai School of Technology is a new-age engineering college focused on providing students with a modern and practical learning environment. The college encourages students to explore technology, participate in hackathons, build projects, and develop skills beyond the classroom. With a growing student community, supportive seniors, and various technical activities, Mirai provides opportunities for students to learn, collaborate, and gain real-world experience. The campus also offers facilities for academics, student activities, and hostel life. Overall, it is a place where students can explore their interests, work on new ideas, and enjoy their college journey while preparing for the future.
